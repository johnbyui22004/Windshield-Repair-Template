Precision Auto Glass ("Precision Auto Glass," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit or use our website, https://precisionautoglass.com (the "Website"), or submit a request for service through the Website.

By using the Website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you voluntarily submit our quote or contact form, we may collect the following information:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • City or service location
  • Type of windshield or glass damage
  • Insurance provider, if voluntarily provided

We collect this information because it is necessary to respond to requests for quotes, answer questions, determine potential service needs, and communicate with customers.

We do not ask you to provide sensitive information such as Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, passwords, payment card numbers, or medical information through the Website. Please do not submit such information through the Website.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information you submit to:

  • Respond to quote and service requests;
  • Contact you by telephone, text message, or email regarding your request;
  • Determine the appropriate windshield repair service;
  • Discuss your vehicle damage and potential repair options;
  • Discuss insurance coverage or billing questions when applicable;
  • Provide, schedule, and manage services;
  • Maintain business and customer records;
  • Protect the Website and business against spam, fraud, abuse, or security threats; and
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use information submitted through the Website for purposes unrelated to the operation of our business unless permitted or required by law or with your consent.

3. How We Share Information

We may share information when reasonably necessary to operate our business and respond to your request.

For example, information submitted through the Website may be processed by third-party technology providers that help us host or operate the Website and process form submissions.

Our Website uses Netlify Forms to process submissions made through the quote request form. Information submitted through that form may be stored and processed through Netlify's services. Netlify provides tools for businesses to access, export, and delete form submissions.

We may also disclose information:

  • To service providers that assist us in operating our business or Website;
  • When necessary to respond to your request or provide requested services;
  • When required by law, legal process, court order, or governmental authority;
  • When reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Precision Auto Glass, our customers, or others; or
  • In connection with a business transfer, merger, sale, or similar transaction, where permitted by law.

We do not sell personal information submitted through the Website for monetary consideration.

4. Form Submissions

Our quote form is intended to allow customers to request information, pricing, or service.

Information submitted through the form is intended for business purposes and may be reviewed by Precision Auto Glass personnel or authorized individuals assisting the business.

Please do not submit information that is unnecessary to your service request.

In particular, do not submit:

  • Social Security numbers;
  • Driver's license numbers;
  • Credit card or bank account numbers;
  • Passwords;
  • Medical information; or

Submitting a phone number or email address does not necessarily mean that Precision Auto Glass will send marketing communications. Communications may be made when reasonably necessary to respond to or fulfill your request.

Standard message and data rates may apply to text messages depending on your mobile carrier.
